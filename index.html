About the network

More than a server. A place to make your mark.

Capital Network was established in July 2024 with one goal: deliver a polished, immersive roleplay experience backed by a growing community and a team that genuinely cares about the city.

Since launch, the server has continued to evolve through community feedback, new systems and ongoing development. The focus remains simple — stable gameplay, enjoyable roleplay and enough freedom for every character to build their own path.
